Important: Back up your experiments. Records are stored locally in this browser, not on a server or in your GitHub account. They usually remain after closing the browser, but may be lost if you clear site data, uninstall or reset the browser, or change phones. Clearing cached images and files alone normally does not delete them. Use Export XML regularly and keep the file somewhere safe (such as cloud storage or another device). On a new phone, use Import XML to restore your records.
